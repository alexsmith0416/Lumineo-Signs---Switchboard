1  Introduction

The Lumineo Project Scheduler is the shop's single, shared home for planning work — replacing the old Canvas scheduling app and the whiteboards, spreadsheets, and guesswork that came with it.

It brings three connected calendars together — Production (the shop floor), Installation (the WK & NEK field crews), and Shipping — all driven by the same scheduling engine and fed live from Business Central (BC). Everyone sees the same board, updated in real time, so the person in the office and the crew lead in the field are always looking at the same plan.

What you can do with it

See the whole week at a glance

Every job, every person, color-coded by department — with weather, crew size, and dollar value right on the card.

Schedule by dragging

Drop a job on a day, stretch it to change its length, and let the engine keep everything downstream in order.

Plan "what-ifs" safely

Try a change in the Scenario Sandbox before it touches the live board.

Hit the monthly number

Roll up installation billing against the goal and auto-fill the month from jobs that are ready.

Who this guide is for Every team member who touches the schedule — from admins who plan it to floor and field staff who read it. Not every section applies to every role; jump to Roles & Permissions to see what your login can do.

2  Getting Started

Opening the app

  1. Open the scheduler link your admin shared with you (it opens in any browser — desktop, tablet, or phone). Bookmark it for one-tap access.
  2. Sign in with your Microsoft / Lumineo account when prompted. That's the same login you use for email.
  3. The app opens on the screen that fits your role — the office team lands on Production; installers land on their Installation region; sales & PMs land on My Active Jobs.
Tip — add it to your home screen On a phone or tablet, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" so the scheduler launches like an app. On a shop TV, turn on Presentation mode (see Settings) for a clean, full-screen board.

Reading the board

  • Rows are grouped under a colored department / location banner (Metal Shop, Paint, Hutchinson, Wichita…). The banner color also tints that group's cards.
  • Columns are the seven days (Mon–Sun). Weekends are shaded because we don't usually schedule them.
  • Cards show the job number, customer, and task; a bar's width is how long the job takes. An arrow (◂ ▸) means the job continues off the visible week or skips the weekend.
  • Week navigation — use ‹ Prev / Next › to move a week at a time, or Today to jump back to the current week.
Tip Hover almost anything — a badge, an icon, a button — and a tooltip explains what it does. When in doubt, hover first.

5  Features & How to Use Them

5.1  Adding a job

Click + Add Job (top-right of the toolbar) or click an empty day cell for a person. You get two kinds of card:

BC Job

  1. Search by BC job number or customer name and pick the job.
  2. Choose a mode: Single (one task), Multi (pick several tasks — they stack on one card), Auto (the engine places every task on the best person/day), or Custom task (type a task for a job that has no BC planning line).
  3. Review the predicted placement, then Schedule.

Custom Card (blocking out time)

For PTO, holidays, inventory, truck maintenance, certifications — anything that isn't a BC job.

  1. Switch to Custom Card and pick a saved card from the dropdown (built-in ones like PTO / Holiday, plus any your team saved), or build your own.
  2. Set the title, color (use the Department / Location swatches to match the group), hours, and scope — One resource, Entire department, or All (e.g. a shop-wide Holiday).
  3. Optionally tick Lock so cascade flows around it, then Schedule.
  4. Reuse it next time by clicking ＋ Save as reusable card — it's saved for this board (Production or Installation) and shared with the team.

5.2  Moving, resizing & the cascade

  • Move — drag a card to another day or person.
  • Resize — drag the right edge of a card to change how long the job takes (hours snap to quarter-hours; a live "Nh" badge shows the new duration).
  • Cascade — when a move/resize would push other jobs, a dialog previews exactly what moves and offers: Continue (cascade the others), Move only this, Try in Sandbox, or Turn cascade off.
Cascade on vs. off With cascade on (default) the board keeps itself conflict-free by nudging downstream work. Turn it off in Settings to move things freely as a full override — overlaps just show a ⚡ icon instead of auto-moving.

5.3  Editing a job

Click a card to open the Edit Job panel. From there you can change the job description and card text, re-pick BC tasks, reassign the employee, set the Start, Hours, or End (setting the end adjusts the duration to land on that date), lock the card, and — on Installation — set trips, men, trucks, and the install ZIP. You can also Duplicate the card onto other people, or Delete it.

5.4  Undo / Redo

Made a mistake? Use the ↶ / ↷ buttons in the week summary, or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Up to 20 steps per board. History clears when the board reloads (e.g., changing weeks).

5.5  The Job Queue

A slide-out holding area (toggle it from the week summary) for jobs that need scheduling but don't have a slot yet. Organize jobs into colored groups ("Needs Scheduled", "Ready to Schedule"…), then drag a card straight onto the board — or drag a scheduled card back into the queue to un-schedule it.

5.6  Roster management (crews & departments)

  • Add a person — right-click a department / location banner → Add employee.
  • Edit / delete — right-click a person's name.
  • Reorder / move — right-click the resource column header to unlock drag-reordering, then drag a name to a new spot or group. You'll be asked "just this week" or "permanent" (see Walkthrough 4).

5.7  Assist Installation (lending people across departments)

Some days a Production person works with an install crew. Right-click their name → Assist Installation, pick the region (WK / NEK) and the days — each day can be All, AM, or PM. Their Production day is greyed for the lent half; the open half still takes a Production job. Come back anytime to Edit days (drop a day or change AM/PM) or Remove assist.

5.8  Scenario Sandbox

A safe copy of the board for "what-if" planning. Make changes, see the impact, and only commit them to the live schedule when you're happy — or discard them. Admin/Ops only.

5.9  Monthly Plan & AI auto-fill

A month roll-up of installation billing across both regions, with per-week cards and a line for the monthly goal. The AI auto-fill button greedily fills the month from the pool of jobs that are ready for install, respecting promised dates and a per-week cap. Admin/Ops only.

5.10  My Schedule / My Active Jobs

Floor and field staff get a focused My Schedule — just their week. Sales & PMs get My Active Jobs — every job they own and where it currently sits across Production, Installation, and Shipping.

6  Step-by-Step Walkthroughs

Walkthrough 1 — Schedule a new BC job, start to finish

  1. Open the Production board and navigate to the target week.
  2. Click + Add Job → keep BC Job selected → search the job number.
  3. Pick the job, choose Auto to let the engine place its tasks (or Single/Multi to place them yourself).
  4. Review the predicted person/day under each task, then click Schedule.
  5. Fine-tune by dragging the card to a different day/person or stretching its right edge; confirm any cascade prompt.

Walkthrough 2 — Block a day of PTO

  1. Click + Add Job → Custom Card.
  2. Pick PTO from the saved-cards dropdown (it comes pre-locked).
  3. Set scope to One resource and choose the person; confirm the day and hours.
  4. Click Schedule. The engine now flows work around that block.

Walkthrough 3 — Lend someone to install for a half-day

  1. On the Production board, right-click the person's name → Assist Installation.
  2. Choose the region (WK or NEK), untick All week, and turn on the day(s).
  3. For a half day, set that day to AM or PM.
  4. Click Assign to Installation. To change it later, reopen and use Edit days.

Walkthrough 4 — Move a crew member for one week only

  1. Right-click the resource column header to unlock drag-reordering (the lock icon opens).
  2. Drag the person's name to the new department / location, or to a new position in the list.
  3. In the prompt, choose This week only (just this week) or Permanent (every week).
Tip "This week only" is perfect for covering a short-handed crew without disturbing the standing roster. Other weeks are untouched.

9  Best Practices

  • Lock the fixed stuff. PTO, holidays, and hard commitments should be locked (🔒) so cascade never moves them.
  • Let Auto do the first pass. Add jobs with Auto mode, then hand-tune — it's faster than placing every task.
  • Prefer "This week only" for temporary coverage. Keep the standing roster stable; use week overrides for one-off shuffles.
  • Use the Sandbox for big changes. Test a re-plan there before committing it live.
  • Keep BC clean. Cards pull customer, value, ZIP, and crew from BC — accurate BC data means accurate cards.
  • One board, one truth. Everyone works the live board; avoid side spreadsheets that drift out of sync.

11  FAQs & Tips

Do my changes save automatically?

Yes. Every move, resize, add, edit, and delete saves to the shared database in the background — there's no "Save" button for the board.

Will other people see my changes right away?

Changes save immediately; teammates see them the next time their board loads or refreshes.

What's the difference between locking a card and turning cascade off?

Locking protects one card. Cascade off is a board-wide mode that stops all auto-moving.

Can I undo after I change weeks?

Undo history is per-board and clears on reload (including week changes), so undo what you need before navigating away.

How do the crew size and weather get on the card?

Crew is derived from the job's BC planning lines (or entered by hand, which wins); weather comes from the install ZIP's forecast.

I'm on my phone — is anything different?

Everything works; the layout adapts. The sidebar becomes a hamburger menu and card details stack vertically.
