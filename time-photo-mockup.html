Mockup Notes

Brand Tokens

  • Navy #141464
  • Navy Light #2a2a8a
  • Red #E8151B
  • Navy BG #e8eaf5
  • Complete pill bg
  • Paint pill bg
  • Assembly pill bg

Screens covered

  • scrDashboard — Home / Today
  • scrPunchIn — Job → Task
  • Clock-out modal w/ "Completed Task"
  • scrCapture — camera + categories
  • scrGallery — per-job photos
  • scrHistory — punches w/ range total
  • scrEditRequest — supervisor email
  • scrAdminExport — Admin CSV export
  • Offline Mode — queued punches/photos

Parity with Sales Hub

  • Same 44 px navy header w/ red logo block + navy-light home button
  • Same navy → navy-light gradient status card pattern (reused for the live punch card)
  • Same 2-col quick-action grid w/ 36 px navy-bg icon circles (Upload Photos is the only red-tinted one)
  • Same status pills, role badges, tight spacing, 10 px button radius
  • Same photo thumbnail timestamp overlay (black bg, white text)

What's Time & Photo-specific

  • Live elapsed-time card with pulsing dot
  • Job → Task selector (no Planning Line picked by employee)
  • Switch-job logic with "Completed previous task" inline checkbox
  • Clock-out modal with "Completed Current Task" decision
  • Photo categories simplified to Survey / Completion
  • Grand total at top of History — totals the active range (Today / Week / Pay Period / Custom)
  • Offline mode — IndexedDB queue (Dexie), GPS-stamped, auto-syncs on reconnect (Linear ALE-69)
  • Pending-punches admin view with CSV export (Phase 1 interim)
